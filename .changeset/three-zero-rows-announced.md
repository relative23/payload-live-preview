The plan for 3.0 now names five more changes, so they are known before the release. A hook has to name its `document`, a merge gets a default deadline, Node 22 becomes the floor, and the `payload` peer range becomes `^3 || ^4`, with Payload 5 arriving in a 3.x minor. No framework major leaves with 3.0. Nothing changes in 2.x. These are entries 26 to 30 of the ADR 0007 ledger; docs/migration.md says how to prepare for each.
