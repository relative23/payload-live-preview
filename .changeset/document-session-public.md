`DocumentSession` is exported from `payload-live-preview/core`, as `@beta`, and the SvelteKit guide has a store recipe on it. The session behind the React and Vue hooks needs no framework, but a Svelte component that renders from a document had no way to reach it. The options guide now says that every option in its table is Stable, and the bindings guide says what to do about a conditional element, a choice between two fields, and a field with no translation.
