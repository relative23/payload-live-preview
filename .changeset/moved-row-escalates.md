A binding on an index path goes to the fragment or route strategy (`LP0411`) when another row now stands at its position, going by the rows' `id`. The patch moved the title into the first element, and the image and other markup without a binding stayed with the old row. Rows without an `id` are not judged, and the lean profile, which has no strategies, leaves the check out.
